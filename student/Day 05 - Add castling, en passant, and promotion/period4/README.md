# Day 5, Period 4: Promotion, and Remus plays in En Croissant

Register `remus.bat` from this folder as an engine in En Croissant and play your own Remus. It plays random legal moves, and every special move works.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this period, each pair can:

- Promote a pawn to any of four pieces. **Measured by:** The promotion tests pass for queen, rook, bishop, and knight.
- Play Remus in En Croissant. **Measured by:** The pair registers `remus.bat`, plays one game, and sees a random legal reply to every move, special moves included.
